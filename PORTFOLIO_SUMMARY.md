# Portfolio Summary

## Retail Deep Dive
This project analysed 400,947 cleaned UK retail transactions worth £8.80M to show how Python, SQL, and Power BI can be used together in a commercially useful workflow. The output was not just descriptive reporting: it identified a 67.06% repeat purchase rate, segmented customers into actionable RFM groups, and highlighted 460 Champion customers for targeted retention. From a business perspective, it shows the ability to turn raw transaction data into decisions on customer value, campaign timing, and international market focus.

## AI Marketing Pipeline
This project built an end-to-end sentiment reporting pipeline for customer reviews and simulated social posts so a marketing team could monitor customer opinion without manual review. It compared VADER with DistilBERT, selected DistilBERT for stronger negative-sentiment detection, and produced automated reports plus Power BI-ready outputs across 1,500 combined records. The business value is speed and early warning: the workflow helps a team surface complaint themes faster, respond to sentiment deterioration earlier, and reduce the manual effort needed for recurring reporting.

## ROI Optimizer
This project built a Marketing Mix Model to estimate which advertising channels generate the strongest return and how a future budget should be allocated. Using adstock, OLS regression, ROI calculations, and scenario analysis, the model showed TV returning £1.06 per £1 spent while removing it would reduce predicted sales by 58.50%. The commercial value is direct budget decision support: instead of relying on instinct, a stakeholder can use the model to compare scenarios, reduce inefficient spend, and justify allocation changes with evidence.
